# ProofLink Research & Strategy — Master Plan

## Mission
Build the definitive research base and product strategy for ProofLink — an agentic payment trust layer that is differentiated from everything currently in market.

## Research Phases

### Phase 1: Deep Technology Research (Parallel)
- **x402 Protocol** — HTTP 402 payment-required protocol, how it works, implementations
- **ERC-8004** — Token standard, compliance, on-chain invoicing
- **Visa/Stripe/Traditional Finance Innovation** — Visa's crypto moves, Stripe's stablecoin play, embedded finance
- **Agentic Payment Systems** — AI agent-to-agent payments, autonomous transactions
- **Request Finance & Competitors** — Request Network, Superfluid, Sablier, Utopia Labs, Coinshift, etc.

### Phase 2: Competitive Analysis & Gap Identification
- Map every competitor's features
- Identify what NO ONE is doing
- Find the whitespace

### Phase 3: ProofLink Product Strategy
- Unique value proposition
- Technical architecture
- Go-to-market differentiation
- Funding narrative

### Phase 4: Review & Refinement
- Cross-review all research
- Stress-test assumptions
- Improve and iterate
