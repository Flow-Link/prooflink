## 1. Coinbase Partnership

### Program: Coinbase Partner Network (CPN)

The Coinbase Partner Network is a vetted ecosystem for companies building end-to-end Coinbase-powered solutions. It is distinct from Coinbase Ventures (equity) — this is a go-to-market integration program.

**Two partner tracks:**
- **Technology Partners** — integrate Coinbase APIs directly into their platform for end-clients (ProofLink's fit)
- **Consulting Partners** — build custom solutions for end-clients using Coinbase APIs

**Benefits (confirmed):**
- Direct communication channel to Coinbase sales and onboarding teams
- Technical consulting with Solution Architecture team
- Early access to developer tooling, API testing, and product roadmaps

**How to Apply:**
- URL: https://www.coinbase.com/partners → "Get in Touch" form
- Applications are open; there is no public waitlist
- First cohort included: thirdweb, Fundraise Up, Mojito, Lazer Technologies, Bitwave, Crossmint

**ProofLink's angle:** ProofLink fits the Technology Partner track. Lead with the compliance-first stablecoin payments narrative (Travel Rule + USDC + enterprise AR/AP). Bitwave is already in — ProofLink can position as the payment execution layer that feeds Bitwave's accounting.

### Program: x402 Ecosystem Partner

x402 is Coinbase's open HTTP payment protocol (HTTP 402 revival). The x402 Foundation is co-led by Coinbase and Cloudflare. The ecosystem page lists 300+ projects across infrastructure, facilitators, client-side, and services.

**How to Submit:**
- Website: https://www.x402.org/ecosystem
- Contact form available in site navigation ("Contact" → Google Form)
- GitHub contributions also accepted: https://github.com/coinbase/x402
- Maintained by Coinbase Developer Platform; submissions reviewed by CDP team

**Current notable x402 ecosystem partners:** Alchemy, AWS, Cloudflare, Stripe, thirdweb, 1Pay.ing, Primer

**ProofLink's angle:** Apply as a Services/Endpoints partner — "ProofLink adds Travel Rule compliance and multi-currency settlement to x402 payment flows." This creates a technical hook where any API monetized via x402 can route through ProofLink for compliant settlement.

### Key Contacts to Identify

No specific named partnership lead was surfaced publicly for CDP. Recommended approach:
- DM `@CoinbaseDev` on X (their official developer platform account)
- Submit via https://www.coinbase.com/partners form
- LinkedIn search: "Partner Manager" + "Coinbase Developer Platform" — there is a posted role for "Manager, Partner Management, Business Development" at Coinbase
- Attend CDP hackathons and builder events — CDP Ambassador Program is invite-only but exposure path

**Coinbase Developer Platform X:** https://x.com/CoinbaseDev

## 3. Circle Partnership

### Program A: Circle Alliance Program

**Purpose:** Companies with live solutions built on Circle's platform (USDC/EURC), serving the stablecoin ecosystem as a service provider.

**Eligibility:**
- Must have a live solution built with Circle's platform
- Must support USDC or EURC
- Must operate as a service provider for the stablecoin ecosystem

**Application link:** https://onboarding.circle.com/signup/alliance

**Benefits:** Global ecosystem visibility, co-marketing, technical support access

**ProofLink's fit:** Apply once MVP is live and processing USDC. Strong fit as a payment orchestration + compliance layer for USDC flows.

### Program B: Circle Payments Network (CPN)

**Launched:** April 21, 2025

**What it is:** A regulated network of banks, PSPs, VASPs, and enterprises enabling 24/7 real-time cross-border settlement via USDC and EURC.

**Scale:** Design partners include 27+ companies across fintech and payments (Alfred Pay, BCB Group, BVNK, CoinMENA, Coins.ph, Conduit, dLocal, dtcpay, Flutterwave, FOMO Pay, HIFI, Inswitch, Legend Trading, Nilos, Noah, Nuvei, Onafriq, OpenPayd, RD Technologies, RedotPay, TazaPay, Transfero Group, Triple-A, Trubit, Unlimit, Yellow Card, Zepz, Zodia Markets). Banking advisors: Banco Santander, Deutsche Bank, Société Générale, Standard Chartered.

**Eligibility:** Only financial institutions meeting CPN eligibility requirements per jurisdiction can join. Must submit an RFI describing flow of funds, capabilities, and business needs. Must obtain USDC via Circle Mint, crypto exchange, or OTC desk.

**How to Apply:** https://www.circle.com/cpn → "Join the network" → `/cpn/interest` form

**Key Circle Contact:**
- **Nikhil Chandhok** — Chief Product & Technology Officer, Circle. Leads CPN product direction. LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/chandhok/ (confirmed active)

**ProofLink's angle for CPN:** Position as a "payment orchestration" node that helps CPN participants onboard enterprise clients who need Travel Rule compliance, multi-currency settlement, and accounting integration on top of raw USDC rails. Could join as a network participant enabling B2B payment flows for corporate treasury customers.

**Recommended sequence:**
1. Apply to Circle Alliance Program now (once MVP live)
2. Build CPN integration/technical readiness in parallel
3. Pursue CPN membership after regulatory licensing/MSB registration is in place
4. Use CPN partner list (BVNK, Yellow Card, Flutterwave, etc.) as ProofLink's first enterprise customer pipeline
